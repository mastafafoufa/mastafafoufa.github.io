Website structure and maintenance

Purpose
Present applied AI work, independent projects, and published writing through
concrete contributions and independently attributed coverage. Keep the
black-and-white editorial design; retain the original book-cover artwork.

Main website
The active homepage is /en/. The opening order is introduction, News &
Conversations, book, currently building, and Microsoft impact. Writing,
research, teaching/background, and contact follow.

Describe Microsoft work through evaluation representativeness, shared
frameworks, and product decision-making. Publish adoption, user, efficiency,
or financial figures only with a defined scope, evidence, and disclosure
permission. Do not describe patent applications as granted patents or
assume that an invention caused a product's revenue.

Use the official book subtitle and published-author wording. Keep the cover
clickable and the publisher purchase link easy to find.

Languages and recoverable source
The root entry point and /fr/ redirect to /en/. French homepage anchors are
mapped to their English equivalents. Non-blog French press routes redirect
to their English reference pages.

The French homepage, French press reference, and unfinished article source
are retained under _archive at their original relative paths. The legacy
blog hub is preserved separately at _archive/legacy/blog/index.html.
_config.yml excludes _archive from GitHub Pages. This is source
preservation, not private storage: repository source remains accessible,
and a plain local Python server does not apply Jekyll exclusions.

To restore a parked page, copy its archived source back to its original
route, replacing the redirect. Review its copy and links, then restore its
navigation, canonical/alternate metadata, and sitemap entries together.

Bilingual blog
Keep /en/blog/ and /fr/blog/ active. Both languages link Home to /en/.
Language switches link to the same article's translation only when one
exists. French-only essays do not advertise an English translation.

Only articles with content appear in the hubs, assets/js/article-nav.js,
and sitemap.xml. Old unfinished-article URLs are noindex redirects to their
language's blog hub. When publishing a preserved draft, replace its redirect
and update the hub, article navigation, and sitemap together.

News & Conversations
Keep a single featured item without carousel controls. Add future .news-card
articles inside #news-items, giving each its own heading ID and
aria-labelledby reference. Include the outlet/host, format, date, language,
title, original source, and credits. assets/js/news-rail.js enables previous/
next buttons and keyboard navigation when multiple cards exist; native
horizontal scrolling supports touch. There is no autoplay.

Press references retain original summaries and source/archive-search links.
Do not mirror full articles or photographs without permission or imply an
archive snapshot exists when only a search link is available.

Medium and research
Keep selected Medium links visible, with older articles in a native details
disclosure. The follower figure is manually maintained, not a live counter.
Patent-title links lead to Google Scholar; US patent and application numbers
are identified separately. The thesis title links directly to its PDF.

Local review
From the repository root, run:
  python -m http.server 8000 --bind 127.0.0.1
Open http://127.0.0.1:8000/en/.
Review desktop/mobile layouts, menu access after scrolling, keyboard focus,
native disclosures without JavaScript, redirects, blog translations,
source/book/research links, and sitemap destinations before deployment.
