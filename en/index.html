About & teaching

I trained as an engineer at Télécom Paris and hold a master’s in Machine Learning from KTH Stockholm. My work at Microsoft spans NLP, AI evaluation, and human-computer interaction.

From 2021 to 2023, I taught advanced NLP and large language models at EPITA Paris. Teaching, my Medium articles, and La paresse de penser share the same aim: making technical ideas understandable without losing the difficult questions.

Raised in Nîmes, with Moroccan roots, I’m now based in Seattle. Outside work, I hold a black belt in Judo, am a former two-time national champion in Karate Jutsu, and surf in the Pacific Northwest.
