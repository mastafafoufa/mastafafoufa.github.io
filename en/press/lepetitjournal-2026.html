My summary

The interview follows my journey from France to Seattle and the connections between my work in AI at Microsoft, teaching and writing. It explores how scientific rigour and a desire to share knowledge have shaped that path.

We also discuss La paresse de penser and the question behind it: how can we make use of intelligent tools while continuing to exercise our own judgement? This is my own summary, not a reproduction or translation of the article.
